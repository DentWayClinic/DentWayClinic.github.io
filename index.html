Talk to Our Team

Your Dental Visit Starts With a Conversation

Planning dental treatment abroad can raise many questions. We can help you understand the next steps before you travel.

Share a recent panoramic X-ray or CBCT scan, if available, along with your goals. A qualified dental professional can assess your case and advise on suitable options.

  • Individual case review and treatment discussion
  • Dental imaging support where appropriate
  • Help coordinating travel and accommodation enquiries
  • Communication with our team before your visit
How It Works

Your Journey to Tirana

We can help you plan your visit step by step, from your first enquiry to discussing aftercare with your dental team.

01. Contact Us

Tell us about your goals and share available dental records or X-rays securely.

02. Case Review

Your information can be reviewed to discuss possible treatment options and next steps.

03. Plan Your Visit

Discuss appointment dates and any travel or accommodation coordination you may need.

04. Visit the Clinic

Your dentist will assess you in person and confirm the appropriate treatment plan.
