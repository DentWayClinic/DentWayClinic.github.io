USEFUL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a consultation?

Contact us via WhatsApp or the enquiry form. You can tell us about your needs and ask how to share relevant dental records securely.

Can you estimate treatment costs before I travel?

We can discuss an initial estimate based on the information provided. Final treatment recommendations and costs require clinical assessment and may change after an in-person examination.

Can you help with travel planning?

Ask our team about available support for appointment scheduling and travel or accommodation enquiries. Availability should be confirmed before making arrangements.

Is every patient suitable for implants?

No. Suitability depends on individual oral health, medical history, examination and diagnostic imaging. A qualified dental professional must determine the appropriate options.
